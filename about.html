Where it started

My name means translation. Turns out, so does my job.

Turns out it's the same job I do now. A product doesn't sell itself just because the founder understands it. Someone has to translate why it matters into something a stranger will actually feel. Your customers already do that, in their own words. I help you capture it.

How I work

What you can expect from me.

1I research before I record

Before any interview or video, I look at what your competitors already say about themselves, so your customer stories fill the gap they leave open.

2Your customers stay comfortable

Short, friendly calls. No scripts. Nothing goes live without their OK, and nobody is surprised to see their own face online.

3Real deadlines, kept

Clear scope, clear dates. I don't wait for inspiration, I work to the due date we agree on.

4Smaller scope, not cheaper work

Not ready for a full plan? We start with a smaller piece, like a few customer calls or one case study, done properly.
