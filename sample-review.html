Why customers buy Attio.

Attio's homepage leads with AI agents. Its customers lead with "it's simple" and "it fits how we work." AI comes up in most reviews, but almost never as the reason they bought.

Not a client. I don't work with Attio and haven't spoken to its customers. This sample is built from 26 public reviews and replies on G2, Product Hunt and a RevOps forum, read October 31, 2026. A real Why They Buy sprint uses confidential calls with won, lost and churned customers.

The gap

What the homepage says vs. what customers say.

Attio's homepage says

"Welcome to agentic revenue."

  • Agents build pipeline and move deals "around the clock"
  • For "the people who own the number"
  • Nothing about what you're switching from
Customers say

It's simple, and it fits how we work.

  • Clean and fast; AI is a nice extra
  • Often ops leads, marketers and SDRs on small teams
  • They're comparing it with HubSpot: too expensive, too heavy

What I'd do differently

Lead with why people switch. Let the agents be the upgrade.

Headline: "Welcome to agentic revenue." → "The CRM that fits how you work. Agents do the busywork."

Subheadline: agents working around the clock → build it your way, set up in minutes, agents do the research and updates

Top bar, badge and button: a path for people switching from HubSpot, and the G2 rating as proof

  1. Lead with the reason people switch

    Fit and simplicity first, agents second. Keep the agents section; move it below the reason people buy.

  2. Name the switch

    A clear path for people leaving HubSpot, with a migration guide right next to it.

  3. Answer the three big worries on the page

    Reporting, integrations and the first week, each with proof: a real dashboard, the integration count, a setup template.
