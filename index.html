FAQ

Questions founders ask me

Can’t I just use AI for this?

AI can write copy. It can’t call your customers. LLMs trust what real customers say on sites you don’t own: reviews, videos, communities. That part needs a human.

Do my customers have to be on camera?

No. Every interview becomes written reviews and stories first. Video is only for customers who want it.

What do you need from me?

A 30-min kickoff and intros to 5 to 10 customers who already love you. I handle the rest, and you approve everything before it goes live.

When will I see something?

First reviews and videos go live in the first month. Search and LLM visibility builds over the months after.

Who is this for?

Seed to Series A tech startups with happy customers and no marketer yet.
