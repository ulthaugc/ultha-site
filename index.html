FAQ

Questions founders ask me

Can’t I just use AI for this?

AI can write copy. It can’t call your customers. LLMs trust what real customers say on sites you don’t own: reviews, videos, communities. That part needs a human.

Do my customers have to be on camera?

No. Every interview becomes written reviews and stories first. Video is only for customers who want it.

What do you need from me?

A 30-min kickoff and intros to 5 to 10 customers who already love you. I handle the rest, and you approve everything before it goes live.

When will I see something?

First reviews and videos go live in the first month. Search and LLM visibility builds over the months after.

Who is this for?

Growing software teams with customers who love them, no marketer yet, and buyers who research before they ever talk to sales.

How is this different from hiring an agency?

You work with me directly, start to finish. No account managers, no hand-offs, no retainer with no end date. Every plan has a clear scope and timeline.

What if my customers say no?

Most say yes when it's a short, friendly chat about their work, not a testimonial request. If someone isn't comfortable, nothing is published. We only use what they're happy with.

Do you only do testimonial videos?

No. Every customer call becomes written testimonials, case studies, reviews and short-form content. Video is one format, not the whole thing.

Can you make UGC-style videos for ads too?

Yes. I make creator-style short videos for ads and social as well, alongside the real customer stories.

Will this help us show up in AI search?

That's a big part of it. AI tools lean on reviews, case studies and content on sites you don't own. I build that proof and optimize your pages so AI has real things to cite.

Can I book just one thing, like a case study?

Yes. Case studies, short-form videos, customer interviews and AI search optimization can all be booked on their own. See the add-ons on the pricing page.

How soon can we start?

Usually within a week. We do a 30-minute kickoff, you send a few customer intros, and the first interviews happen right after.
